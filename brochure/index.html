01Welcome

The art of
hospitality

At Kanaar, hospitality is a way of being. It begins with a garland at the door, a cool drink after a long drive, and the quiet assurance that everything has been thought of.

Set in Peerumadara, near the Kosi river and the sal forests of Jim Corbett, Hotel Kanaar Corbett brings together 42 rooms and suites, a restaurant rooted in the flavours of Uttarakhand, and event spaces made for life's larger moments. It is a place to arrive as a guest and leave as part of the family.

08The Experience

The wild is
your neighbour.

Jim Corbett National Park lies at the edge of Ramnagar, with the Kosi tracing the valley and sal forests holding the morning mist. Days here begin with birdsong and end with warm light.

01

Forest mornings

Mist in the sal trees, birdsong before breakfast, and the particular quiet of the Uttarakhand foothills at first light.

02

River country

The Kosi runs through Ramnagar, tracing the edge of the terai. Days here are measured in water, light and long lunches.

03

Slow evenings

Return from the wild to warm light, a table set for everyone you love, and nowhere else to be.

Some places are visited. Others are returned to.
